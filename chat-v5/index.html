What's in this version

  • Jarvis is a WhatsApp chat. In WhatsApp you Forward messages to it; in any other app you tap Share.
  • Jarvis replies with a one-line summary and a card showing what would change.
  • Nothing changes until you tap. Every change has Undo, and Open calendar jumps to that day.
  • Before signing you up for Career Night, Jarvis shows exactly what it will send. A fake sender gets a warning instead.
  • Same calendar events as chat v4.
