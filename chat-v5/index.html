What's in this version

  • Jarvis lives in WhatsApp. Inside WhatsApp you Forward; from any other app (Telegram, Gmail, Slack…) you use Share, then pick Jarvis.
  • Every capture gets one summary sentence and one card: old → new or new event, clash check, and a status (Needs your OK → Updated).
  • Nothing changes until you tap. Every result has Undo and Open calendar, which opens the iPhone Calendar on that day.
  • Registering for Career Night shows exactly what Jarvis will send first. A look-alike sender gets a warning and no résumé.
  • The calendar uses the same events as chat v4.

A class project mockup. App names describe where the flow happens; not affiliated with or endorsed by WhatsApp, Telegram, Google or Apple. WhatsApp's Business terms limit general-purpose AI chatbots, so a real launch would need policy review. People and messages are made up. Profile photos are open-licensed scikit-image samples (cat, coffee, motorcycle, rocket, moon); other contacts use default avatars.
